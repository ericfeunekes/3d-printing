# Air-vortex cannon attribution

Original design: **egordv, Air Vortex Cannon**, [MakerWorld model 442826, designer profile 348811](https://makerworld.com/en/models/442826-air-vortex-cannon#profileId-348811). Original assembly demonstration: [designer's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKO3LCNGTJY).

The source page was captured under [Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial–ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Original meshes remain in `source/egordv/`; the inventory and import manifest preserve file hashes. Filenames containing `STEP.stl` are triangulated meshes, not native STEP files.

Modifications prepared for Eric Feunekes with Codex, October 25–26, 2026:

- A: a small keeper experiment, not promoted after independent challenges.
- B: continuous shell reinforcement and six keyed shoulder-bolt connections.
- C: three lower conical reinforcement/adapter connections, retained original upper rail/pins and grip, plus a rejected rearward-handle comparison.

These derivatives retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as recorded in their owning handoffs. No separately restricted community lock geometry was incorporated. Attribution does not imply endorsement by the original designer.
